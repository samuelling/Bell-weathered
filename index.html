Space and air

How the sky shapes the sound

Each slider scales how far that part of the sky pulls the sound away from neutral. Zero ignores it, one is as designed, two exaggerates it. A held (manual) value always applies exactly as set.

Weather feeds the sound continuously: warm air opens the filter, wind sweeps it and stirs the gust layer, humidity thickens the glide and the damp, pressure widens the detune, cloud darkens and thickens the space, rain and snow set how often notes fall, the sun sets the harmonic spread, and the moon sends occasional slow swells up from the sub bass. Live weather comes from Open-Meteo and needs this page to be hosted on the open web; inside a sandboxed viewer it runs on a simulated sky instead.
